The Rolling Pearl

Ride the Pearl of Africa

BrandingWebsitesContent

The Challenge

An overland travel brand with an extraordinary product and no digital presence strong enough to carry it.

The Insight

Travelers don't book a vehicle. They book a journey they can already picture themselves on.

The Strategy

Lead with the route, not the service. Build the brand around the experience of the Pearl of Africa.

The Idea

Ride the Pearl of Africa — one journey-led narrative running through identity, content and the website.

The Execution
